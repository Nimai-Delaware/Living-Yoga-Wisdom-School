What is Living Yoga
Wisdom School?

Living Yoga Wisdom School is a structured online school rooted in the philosophy and practice of bhakti — the yoga of devotion. Our curriculum is carefully sequenced to take students from first questions to deep understanding, one course at a time.

The Curriculum

Nine Blocks.
Thirty-Five Courses.

The full curriculum is organized into nine thematic blocks, each building on the last. With that said, each course is also designed to stand on its own, so feel free to explore freely.

Block 1

Introduction to Bhakti Yoga

Core practices and underpinning philosophy — mantra meditation, kirtan, daily spiritual habits, and an exploration of Divinity.

Block 2

Applied Philosophy I: The Four Questions

A framework for turning ordinary experience into spiritual growth through practical reflection and self-honesty.

Block 3

Going Deeper into the Practice I

Deepening japa, kirtan, hearing scripture, association, and Deity worship — from inspiration into rhythm.

Block 4

Philosophy I: The Architecture of Reality

The soul, karma, the modes of nature, suffering, and freedom — Vedic philosophy made humane and accessible.

Block 5

Applied Philosophy II: The Four Pillars of Dharma

How restraint supports freedom — the Four Pillars as practical supports for clarity, integrity, and presence.

Block 6

Philosophy II: Context, Revelation, and Ultimate Reality

Theology, lineage, and how revelation, reason, and experience work together in the Vedic tradition.

Block 7

Going Deeper into Practice II

Mentorship, introspection, navigating doubt, and sustaining a life of devotion long after initial enthusiasm fades.

Block 8

Relationships & Community

Community life, communication, conflict, partnership, and building a healthy spiritual home.

Block 9

Leadership & Service

Leading kirtan, teaching, conflict resolution, and building outreach programs — service as stewardship.
